Meeting held on August 13, 2025. Published on October 14, 2026.

The board discussed youth outreach, fundraising, and public communications.

<!-- more -->

## Community outreach and communications

The discussion explored ways to introduce young people to hacking, including robotics clubs and the online spaces they already use.

The board discussed a fundraising auction to benefit PDXHF and the preparation of a media kit with style guidelines, logos, quotable background material, and a collection of coverage. A workplace-giving registration was also suggested.
