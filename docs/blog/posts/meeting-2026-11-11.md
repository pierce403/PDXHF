Meeting held on November 11, 2026. Published on November 11, 2026.

The board reviewed the year's grant plans, confirmed payment of the PCR workshop grant, and discussed upcoming community activities.

## Grant program update

PDXHF's plan remains five grants for 2026, with three approved and two still to award. One previously approved grant remained unpaid at the time of the meeting. No new grant approvals were recorded in these notes.

The notes record that the [PCR Workshop + Ongoing grant](meeting-2026-10-14.md#grant-2026-pcr-workshop) has been paid. The board also discussed the upcoming biohacking workshop.

[View the grants timeline](../../grants/index.md).

## Community activities and communications

The board discussed possible follow-up public-records instruction building on the earlier FOIA workshop.

The discussion also considered a blog post about how previous grants have supported the hacker ethos of improving things and sharing knowledge, alongside developments in AI and cybersecurity.

## Organizational planning

The board reviewed operating costs, outstanding commitments, and cash reserves. It agreed to maintain roughly a year of operating cash and review grantmaking capacity periodically.

Moving future meetings to a quieter side room at Ctrl+H was suggested, but no motion was made.
