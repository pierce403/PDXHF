## Grant approval: ADS-B surveillance-flight tracking {#grant-2025-adsb}

**Approved:** December 10, 2025.

PDXHF approved funding for this proposed work.

**What it does:** Develop a low-cost, open-source radio system to detect possible surveillance flights and alert Portland-area community groups.

**Why we funded it:** Accessible, open-source tools can help Portland communities understand surveillance flights and learn radio research.
