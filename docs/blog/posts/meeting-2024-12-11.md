Meeting held on December 11, 2024. Published on October 14, 2026.

The board discussed PDXHF’s bylaws and approved steps to establish the foundation’s operations.

<!-- more -->

This is a curated summary of the recorded meeting discussion and decisions.

## Board business

- Reviewed draft bylaws, including how to remove inactive officers and board members.
- Discussed how future changes to annual funding allocations should be approved by the board.
- Approved a dedicated Google Drive for meeting records and an independent PDXHF Google account.
- Agreed to announce future meetings through the PDXHF Signal group.
- Authorized the creation of operating and investment accounts.

## Follow-up work

- Complete the foundation’s charity registration filings.
- Update the website to use the name “PDX Hacker Foundation.”
- Move the meeting records into the foundation’s independent account.
- Meet again on January 8, 2025.
