Meeting held on May 13, 2026. Published on October 14, 2026.

The board reviewed grant timing and clarified how applicants should provide contact information.

## Grant program update

The board reviewed the two grants approved so far in 2026: the FOIA workshop in February and Android TV device research in April. The meeting notes record that the FOIA grant had been paid and payment for the Android TV research was pending as of May 13.

The board discussed plans for three more grants during 2026 and grouping the remaining applications into review cycles of two to three months.

## Application contact information

The website’s contact field was revised to ask applicants for their specific username on their preferred contact platform, so PDXHF can reach them about their proposal.
