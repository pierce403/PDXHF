Meeting held on May 14, 2025. Published on October 14, 2026.

The board discussed how to share funded work and keep the community informed about PDXHF.

<!-- more -->

This is a curated summary of the recorded meeting discussion and decisions.

## Recorded discussion

- Considered ways for future projects to share their work, such as public repositories and project journals.
- Discussed a PDXHF blog featuring grantee updates and local events, along with longer-form posts.
- Discussed cards, stickers, and a tagline for outreach.
- Considered an email list for people who want occasional updates.
- Discussed matching funds, collaboration with other funders, and whether to expand the foundation’s geographic focus.

The available notes cover part of the meeting and end while discussion was still in progress.
