## Grant approval: Android TV device research {#grant-2026-android-tv}

**Approved:** April 8, 2026.

PDXHF approved funding for this proposed work.

**What it does:** Investigate pre-installed software on commodity Android TV streaming devices and develop a reusable kit for security research and learning.

**Why we funded it:** Research into commonly used streaming devices can produce reusable security knowledge and hands-on learning opportunities.

Project link not yet available.

The discussion proposed a write-up and a repository documenting progress as deliverables. These were plans at the time of approval.
