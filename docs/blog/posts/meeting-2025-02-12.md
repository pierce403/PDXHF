Meeting held on February 12, 2025. Published on October 14, 2026.

The board unanimously approved two PDXHF research grants and discussed the funding cycle and public outreach.

<!-- more -->

This is a curated summary of the recorded meeting discussion and decisions.

## Board business

- Approved the initial grants for Cellebrite reverse engineering and in-school monitoring research.
- Discussed a funding cycle with two rounds per year, timed to support project development and possible presentations at local hacker events.
- Received confirmation of nonprofit status from the donation processor.
- Discussed restoring the grant submission form, improving website contact options, and creating stickers.
- Reviewed ongoing tax filing work.

## Grant approvals

### Cellebrite kit reverse engineering { #grant-2025-cellebrite }

Approved on February 12, 2025.

**What it does:** Reverse engineer a Cellebrite forensic kit to understand how it works.

**Why we funded it:** Independent analysis of forensic devices can help the hacker community better understand how these tools work.

Project link not yet available.

### In-school Halo sensor research { #grant-2025-halo }

Approved on February 12, 2025.

**What it does:** Investigate and reverse engineer an in-school Halo monitoring and sensor system.

**Why we funded it:** Research into school monitoring systems can help community members understand surveillance technology in educational settings.

Project link not yet available.
